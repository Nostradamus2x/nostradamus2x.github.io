At Village Capital I led a $1M equity portfolio across three financial inclusion companies in South Asia, running the full cycle from sourcing through due diligence, investment-thesis development, and presentation to the investment committee.

The first investment I led was in [Haqdarshak](https://haqdarshak.com/), a platform that helps people access government welfare schemes and financial services. Diligence took me to a village in rural India to meet the company's field agents and beneficiaries — a useful corrective to evaluating impact from a spreadsheet.

Alongside the investing work, I managed five accelerator programs for 50+ startups across South and Southeast Asia, sourced and evaluated 500+ companies, and built a network of 200+ founders, investors, and sector experts. I also helped set up female-led accelerator programs spanning Afghanistan, Nepal, Bangladesh, and Sri Lanka.
