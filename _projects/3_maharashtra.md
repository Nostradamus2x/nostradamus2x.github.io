As a Chief Minister's Fellow with the Government of Maharashtra, I worked at the intersection of entrepreneurship policy and crisis response.

On the policy side, I drafted the monitoring-and-review section of the Maharashtra State Startup Policy 2020 and led a partnership with the Udhyam Foundation to embed entrepreneurial thinking into the vocational curriculum for roughly 140,000 students a year.

When COVID-19 hit Mumbai, the work shifted. I helped facilitate around $4M in donations to government hospitals, built daily hospital data dashboards and inter-state migrant-movement trackers, and prepared recommendation reports on hospital capacity, triaging, and ambulance services.
