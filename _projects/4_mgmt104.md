As a Teaching Fellow for MGMT E-104 (Introduction to Quantitative Methods for Economics and Finance) at Harvard Extension School, I built a set of interactive tools to accompany each week's material — the kind of thing that's hard to convey through slides alone.

The six tools, all live at [nostradamus2x.github.io/MGMT104E](https://nostradamus2x.github.io/MGMT104E/):

- **Cross-Validation Explainer** — interactive step-through of 2-fold, k-fold, and leave-one-out cross-validation
- **Normal Distribution Visualizer** — shape and area, z-scores, and the 68–95–99.7 rule
- **Regression Explorer** — draw your own data, explore OLS mechanics, and see R² respond in real time
- **Portfolio & CAPM Simulator** — two-asset portfolio construction, the efficient frontier, and the Security Market Line
- **Logistic Regression Explainer** — side-by-side linear vs. logistic comparison, log-odds to probability converter
- **Confusion Matrix Explorer** — model tradeoff visualization across different classification thresholds

The course covers statistics and applied finance for a mixed audience — some students come in comfortable with Excel, others don't. The tools were designed to build intuition first, letting students manipulate inputs and see the math respond, before working through the mechanics formally.
