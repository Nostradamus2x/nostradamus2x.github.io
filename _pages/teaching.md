I served as a Teaching or Course Assistant for several courses at the Harvard Kennedy School and Harvard College, mostly in quantitative methods and applied policy. The role involved running review sessions, holding office hours, and helping students work through problem sets and projects.

**Quantitative and empirical methods**

- Introduction to Quantitative Methods for Economics and Finance (MGMT E-104, Harvard Extension School) — built six [interactive tools](https://nostradamus2x.github.io/MGMT104E/) to accompany the course material
- Randomized Controlled Trials (GOV1737)
- Impact of Data and AI on Politics and Policies (API-205)
- Math Camp (incoming-student preparation)

**Finance and policy**

- Personal Finance (ECON70)
- Public-Private Partnerships

Outside of Harvard, I taught math and visual programming to middle-school students from disadvantaged backgrounds through Tinker Qubits (formerly the Gyanada Foundation), where I remain a fellow and advisor.
